# Changelog

All notable changes to **VikingWards** will be documented in this file.

## [0.1.1] - 2026-10-25

### Fixed
- **Build & Packaging Automation**: Resolved version string escaping in deployment and packaging toolchain.
- **Documentation & Inscriptions**: Corrected runic offering recipe descriptions on inscribed Ward Slates.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-22

### Added
- **Divine Conduits**: Dedicate defensive Wards to one of five Norse patrons (Odin, Thor, Frey, Tyr, Freya) through an interactive inscribed Ward Slate interface.
- **Consecration Offerings**: Deposit thematic offerings (gems, trophies, resin, fine wood) to unlock higher rite tiers within the ward perimeter.
- **Domestic Sanctuary & Deterrence**: Restricts unauthorized door and container tampering while reducing wild predator detection ranges outside walls by 85%.
- **Territory Buffs & Rites**: Attuned wards project passive combat, harvest acceleration, and health/stamina restoration bonuses to defenders within the dome.
